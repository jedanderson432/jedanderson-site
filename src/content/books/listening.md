The verse is mine. The illustrations were generated with AI image tools. Both are offered together as a single artifact — a children's adaptation of the essay [Bits Protect Its](/essays/bits-protect-its/).

This is Book Two in a children's series that began with [We Are Why It Might](/books/we-are-why-it-might/). The first book was about waking up. This one is about listening. A future book, if there is one, will be about reaching.
