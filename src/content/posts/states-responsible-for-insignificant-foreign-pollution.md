EPA now telling the States that they are responsible for offsetting insignificant levels of foreign pollution (see below).

Read the following statements.  How can both be true?

- **“[T]he Clean Air Act allows EPA to approve an ozone attainment plan . . . if . . . international transport of pollution is a significant impediment to meeting the standard****”—EPA Ozone Guidance, October 1, 2015** (see <http://www3.epa.gov/ozonepollution/pdfs/20151001toolsfs.pdf>).

## **vs.**

- **“The Clean Air Act does not hold States responsible for pollution that they do not control****.”—Gina McCarthy and Janet McCabe Congressional Testimony** (see <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=STXIM4OJr80&feature=youtu.be> and <https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=15&v=F04yPwt69d4>).

Based on the 1st statement, States are responsible for offsetting insignificant levels of foreign pollution.   Based on the 2nd, States aren’t responsible for offsetting any foreign pollution.

Which is the truth???

Can’t make States responsible for offsetting foreign pollution–whether its “significant” or “insignificant”.  Not a fair or effective approach to addressing foreign pollution impacts.

Time to transform the Clean Air Act to better prepare ourselves for the problems and opportunities of a 21st century world (ex. “[The Clean Air and Climate Change Act of 2015](/pdfs/sip/Clean_Air_and_Climate_Change_Act_of_2015.pdf)”).  We can make it happen.
