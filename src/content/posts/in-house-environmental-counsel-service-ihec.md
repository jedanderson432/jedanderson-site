The AL Law Group is unveiling it's third of four new "Environmental Legal Products" designed to help clients increase profitability and decrease liability.

Others are operating without environmental counsel.  Still others are looking for supplemental counsel to further enhance internal counsel efforts.  IHEC is designed to satisfy all three needs:
