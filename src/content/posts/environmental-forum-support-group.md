Texas EHS Managers and Environmental Professionals

COVID-19

ENVIRONMENTAL Forum & Support Group

Tuesday, 8:00 am cst ([sharescreen link](http://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001bsxBlmD0xzPQY3RNBRhCPLnbZmJVpAB0rt8svIHGyDKhcby4EZTOmnfuryv5fRooTrzx7FzOKE0L7T6SJNcKOXHV6pT2MrJn7ioapbbHHh04MaKBxGc24Qb-brWfAR5JJFULEDQJ9Qi99Hq1WtOQbcGi16vjBM5b&c=&ch=))

EHS managers, staff, and all environmental professionals are welcome to join an open-forum discussing environmental management and concerns during the Covid-19 response. Please pass this invitation to anyone you feel may be interested.

There is no agenda. This an open-forum for all of you to help each other and help support each other.

This will be an anonymous forum. Please do not say your name or what company you represent. Please use a generic description such as "environmental manager" or consultant or attorney. Views and ideas are your own. I will start it out with a prayer. That is the last time I will speak other than to end the session. Everyone can feel free to share questions, concerns, and ideas. We will have a hard stop at 9:00 am.
