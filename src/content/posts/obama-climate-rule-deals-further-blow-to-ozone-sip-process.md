According to the President and Administrator McCarthy, one of the primary reasons for the new climate rule is to improve air quality.**So this question will blow you away:**

> **What role did the ozone NAAQS/SIP process play in creating a climate rule that has as one of its primary purposes to reduce ozone?**
>
> **Answer:   None.**

**The President is reducing ozone pollution without even touching the ozone NAAQS/SIP process**. 

Amazing to think about isn’t it.

The NAAQS/SIP is becoming less relevant and effective.  I’ve written extensively about this (see [link](http://sipreform.wordpress.com/2012/04/27/distinction-between-ozone-attainment-and-nonattainment-areas-disappearing/) and [link](http://sipreform.wordpress.com/2012/11/09/environmental-law-institute-article-are-state-implementation-plans-becoming-passe/) )—and now the President has dealt the process another blow.  He is completely avoiding it.  Brilliant.

Anyone else starting to get a sense that it’s a “small multi-pollutant world after all”?

The world is changing.  We must change with it.  Time to transform the SIP process.  We can make it happen.
