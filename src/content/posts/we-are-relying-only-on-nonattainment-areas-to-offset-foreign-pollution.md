Look at the map below.  If you live in a nonattainment area you are required to offset foreign pollution. If you live in an attainment you aren’t. Why is our nation relying only on nonattainment areas to address foreign pollution?  If we are going to address foreign pollution by requiring States to offset the pollution with local controls—shouldn’t we at least be fair about it?

The world is changing.  We must change with it.  Time to transform the SIP process.  We can make it happen.
