## (or supplement your current department)

The AL Law Group's In-House Environmental Counsel Service ("IHEC") is a new innovative approach to environmental legal services that provides companies with flexibility, easy access, a predictable low-cost structure, the ability to adjust to meet changing needs, and a whole team of remarkably talented environmental legal experts (see  below).

**IHEC SUMMARY**

- IHEC provides not only one attorney—but a whole environmental legal department
- IHEC provides the flexibility to easily adjust the size of this environmental legal service to meet changing needs
- IHEC provides a dedicated phone line.  Phone will be answered 24 hours per day.
- IHEC can be tailored and used by the smallest company that only needs approx. 5 hours per month--or the largest company that needs in the order of 100 hours per month (or a large company that just wants supplemental services for approx. 5 hours per month)

**IHEC SERVICES**  
 The  following services are available under IHEC:

1. Address questions from attorneys and EHS staff on environmental matters
2. Utilize legal knowledge, experience, and relationships to help clients diagnose and solve environmental problems
3. Use environmental legal knowledge to help the company reduce financial and legal liabilities--and find cost-savings and economic opportunities
4. Work from your site 1-2 days per week if desired
5. Weekly or monthly visit by one of our attorneys to your site if desired
6. Participate in conference calls/meetings on EHS matters
7. Participate in due diligence reviews that contain environmental aspects to the deal
8. Review contracts with environmental components and participate in contract negotiations
9. Provide legal advice, advocacy, and defense on enforcement matters including NOVs, NOEs, Agreed Orders, and other State and Federal actions
10. Undertake assigned legal research and other projects as directed by internal counsel or management [IHEC attorneys each have different skill sets and expertise. Tasks will be assigned based on the client's preference, experience of the attorney on a given matter, and availability.  Attorneys are located in Austin and Houston to cover matters geographically]
11. Assist with legal strategies on environmental authorizations and expediting of these authorizations
12. Review submissions to TCEQ and EPA including deviation reports, NSPS/MACT reports, permit documents, and other agency submissions that potentially create liability and cost to the company.  [Documents will be turned around in 24 hours wherever possible]
13. For any litigation filed or threatened that contains an environmental component, coordinate with internal counsel or other outside counsel to provide initial response and, if authorized, undertake negotiations within approved parameters and file a general denial for an answer [After the answer is filed, evaluate whether the AL Law Group should continue handling the litigation outside of IHEC, within IHEC, or whether another law firm should take over]
14. To the extent desired, coordinate and work with other outside counsel to assure consistency in litigation and arguments on environmental matters
15. Prepare comments on proposed environmental rules or coordinate comments with trade groups
16. Monitor and report on proposed rules that might affect the company or organization
17. Assist with proposing, monitoring, or assessing environmental legislation on the State and Federal levels
18. Review permit applications or renewals
19. Meet with environmental agency representatives as authorized
20. Review and comment on internal and external environmental policy, systems, procedures, and plans
21. Give presentations at EHS training, conferences, and seminars held by client
22. Any other legal service requested within our experience base and within approximate budget
