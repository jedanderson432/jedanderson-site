**Can anyone find a mistake in this reasoning?**

1. At least one molecule of pollution is coming from a foreign country into the United States.
2. SIPs must demonstrate attainment. (42 U.S.C. § 7407(a)).
3. Since SIPs must demonstrate attainment—but are not acknowledging foreign pollution impacts—local citizens and businesses are offsetting foreign pollution impacts with additional controls on local sources.

If there is not a mistake in this reasoning, then why is no one willing to accept this as truth?

> **“Fiction is obliged to stick to possibilities.  Truth isn’t.”**—-Mark Twain
