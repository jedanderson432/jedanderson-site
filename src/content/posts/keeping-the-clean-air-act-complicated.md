**[![The Clean Air Act](/images/sip/the-clean-air-act.png)](/images/sip/the-clean-air-act.png)**Does anyone else sense this?

It feels like there is an unconscious movement to keep the Clean Air Act complicated so that few people understand it other than ourselves.

I keep seeing people die.  And the way they made their living, their reputations, and their stuff doesn’t seem to be going with them from what I can tell.

Might as well simplify the Clean Air Act and try to put ourselves out of business.  Can’t permanently hurt something that’s gonna be dust soon anyway.
