![](http://t3.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcQD6xaXASH2WRMJCqfDCaM8_kgJBDMi8aotn2zU72k15V0zQ_Cm7g "Americans Paying to Offset Foreign Pollution")The Clean Air Act requires States to achieve the national ambient air quality standards (42 U.S.C. § 7407(a)).  A significant portion of the pollution in the U.S. is now coming from foreign countries (see [2010 HTAP report](http://www.htap.org/activities/2010_Final_Report/HTAP%202010%20Part%20A%20110407.pdf) and [NOAA study](http://researchmatters.noaa.gov/news/Pages/ozonestudy.aspx)) .  This means that EPA and the States are now placing additional emission controls on U.S. citizens and businesses in order to offset this foreign pollution and achieve the national ambient air quality standards.

Is this fair?  Is this just?  Is this an effective and efficient way to address foreign pollution?

Time to tell the public the truth about what is happening.  Time to transform the SIP process.  We can make it happen.
