Here is a “Petition for Rulemaking” I filed yesterday on foreign pollution and climate change (see [link](/pdfs/sip/Petition_for_Rulemaking_on_the_Offsetting_of_Foreign_Greenhouse_Gases.pdf)).  I’m hoping it helps brings us further to the realization that we can and need to develop a more coordinated, simpler, and more comprehensive Clean Air Act (see “[Draft Clean Air and Climate Change Act of 2014](http://sipreform.wordpress.com/2014/04/17/the-clean-air-and-climate-change-act-of-2014)“)

We can no longer just think locally and act locally.  And think globally and act locally.  We must also think locally and act globally.  And think globally and act globally.   For indeed . . . it has become a small multi-pollutant world after all.

The world is changing.  We must change with it.  Time to transform the SIP process.  We can make it happen.
