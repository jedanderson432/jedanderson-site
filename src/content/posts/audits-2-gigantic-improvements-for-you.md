AUDITS

TWO GIGANTIC

IMPROVEMENTS

FOR COMPANIES

1. Include External Data Review - If you only look at your own internal data, you are not looking at all the environmental data about your company's environmental performance. You are also more prone to always look good in the eyes of your own data. It's like looking in a mirror and asking yourself if you are good looking. EnviroAI looks at agency data, social media data, internet data, earth data, and other data that can be gathered from the outside to ALSO give you an "outside to inside" perspective on your environmental performance.
2. Implement "Real-Time" Continuous Audits- Audits are "snap-shots" in time. They are static. They essentially are "dated" the day they end. If an environmental problem occurs the next day, that problem might not be assessed for another year or longer. EnviroAI helps companies implement continuous audit functions using artificial intelligence.
