Everything.

![](/images/posts/sacred/img-01.png)

![](/images/posts/sacred/img-02.gif)

I've been told that when I mention God in an email about environmental artificial intelligence . . . I lose credibility.

When I mention love . . . I lose credibility.

When I mention faith. . . I lose credibility.

God, love, and faith are the most powerful forces in the world. To not invoke them in ourselves and others in difficult human problem-solving situations would therefore seem to me to be illogical. Ecclesiastical malpractice. As for credibility, my primary intent is not reputation. My primary intent is to serve God as best as my sinful-self can discern and as fast as my feeble feet can carry me. I will therefore call upon all heavenly and earthly powers I can summon.

---"Everything you do is sacred other than sin."

-Jed Anderson, Creator, EnviroAI
