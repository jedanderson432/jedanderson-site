Jed Anderson of the AL Law Group will be speaking at the 1st Annual Economic Development Luncheon of the Economic Alliance Houston Port Region.  The presentation will focus on how new projects can get environmental authorizations in less time and at less cost.  "Some projects make $100,000 a day" Anderson said.  "If we secure authorizations even 10 days quicker for our clients, we just made them $1 million."

In the last few months the AL Law Group has unveiled "game-changing" environmental legal products that are revolutionizing the environmental legal field.  One of the new products is called "ENPASS" or "Expedited New Projects Authorization Strategies Service" (see [http://www.allawgp.com/legal-products/enpass/).](http://www.allawgp.com/legal-products/enpass/).Â ) Attendees of the luncheon will hear details about some of the underlying tools girding ENPASS and how these tools can catalyze new projects and economic development.

The event will be held on Thursday, May 12th from 11:30 am - 1:00 pm at the Battleground at Deer Park.  To make a reservation, or for more information, please visit the following weblink:  [Economic Alliance Port Region's 1st Annual Economic Development Luncheon](http://events.r20.constantcontact.com/register/event?oeidk=a07eclegiy4f4b4f2b4&llr=7ybhj4sab) or contact Dana Ramirez at dana@allianceportregion.com.
