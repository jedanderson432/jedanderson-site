Sometimes we seem to think of the Clean Air Act as this venerable, imposing, indomitable force.  Just some perspective I wanted to share.  Each one of you is far more powerful and important than the Clean Air Act.  I think we tend to believe the opposite.  This simply is not the case and I will prove it.  Eternity is not promised to statutes and institutions.  Eternity is promised to you.  The Clean Air Act will eventually die.  You won’t.  You are therefore far more powerful and important than the Clean Air Act will ever be.

Improving the Clean Air Act doesn’t seem as imposing anymore does it? . . . especially for a creature who will one day remember the Clean Air Act and the galaxies as an old tale.
