> **———–“The Clean Air Act is not a relic to be displayed in the Smithsonian, but a living document that must be refurbished to continue realizing results. So while the standards I signed today may be strict, we have a responsibility to overhaul and enhance the Clean Air Act to ensure it translates from paper promises into cleaner air.”***– EPA Administrator Stephen Johnson, March 2008*

The world is changing.  We must change with it.  Time to transform the SIP process.  We can make it happen.
