[![EPA and Lemonade](/images/sip/epa-and-lemonade.jpg)](/images/sip/epa-and-lemonade.jpg)No one is seeing the billions of dollars in potential relief EPA is offering States.

On Monday EPA recognized in the final ozone rule that climate change is increasing ozone:

> **“[C]limate change has the potential to increase average summertime O3 concentrations by as much as 1–5 ppb by 2030”** (80 Fed. Reg. 65292 at 65300)
>
> - **81% of CO2 comes from outside the U.S.**
> - **EPA said that States are not responsible for reducing background pollution**
> - **States therefore potentially don’t need to offset 4.05 ppb (i.e. 81% of 5 ppb) with additional local VOC and NOx controls**

**EPA is telling you States that you are not responsible for offsetting the world’s pollution in your SIPs . . . so stop it.  You’ve got enough of your own pollution to deal with.**

The world is changing.  We must change with it.  Time to simplify and transform the Clean Air Act to better prepare ourselves for the problems and opportunities of a 21st century world (ex. “[The Clean Air and Climate Change Act of 2015](/pdfs/sip/Clean_Air_and_Climate_Change_Act_of_2015.pdf)”).  We can make it happen.
