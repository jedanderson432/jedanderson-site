We keep struggling to find middle-ground on climate change and air quality issues.  So . . . let’s stop looking for it.  How about looking for an idea that’s both wildly to the right of Fox News–yet wildly to the left of MSNBC (see example at [link](/pdfs/sip/HBA_Section_-_CAA_Improvements_Presentation_-_January_2014.pdf) and [link](https://docs.google.com/a/jedlaw.net/file/d/0B8c4LpGCMICsZWZlZFljRnpyNFE/edit)). 

Ahh . . . the serendipity of simplicity.

The world is changing.  We must change with it.  Time to transform the SIP process.  We can make it happen.
