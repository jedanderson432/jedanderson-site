## [Sunstein and the Clean Air Act](/images/sip/sunstein-and-the-clean-air-act.jpg)Cass Sunstein’s Book:  “Simpler:  The Future of Government”

- **Reporter’s Question:  “Are there obstacles in the way of simplifying further?”**
- **Cass Sunstein’s Reply:** **“****One big challenge is statutory requirements [i.e., the laws written by Congress]. . . . [S]ometimes efforts to simplify regulations are blocked by legal mandates. That’s a challenge.”**

The Clean Air Act is becoming an impediment to further environmental and economic progress, and in Schoenbrod’s succinct grammatically profundity . . . become just plain “stupid” (see <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iUm16NfK7R8>).

Time now to approach a win-win solution for Democrats and Republicans–and most importantly America.  Time to simplify the Clean Air Act (see “[Clean Air and Climate Change Act of 2014](http://www.sipreform.com/uploads/Clean_Air_and_Climate_Change_Act_of_2014.pdf)”).  We can make it happen.
