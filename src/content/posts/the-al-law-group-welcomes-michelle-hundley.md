Ms. Hundley extends the strategic environmental services of the AL Law Group into the areas of government relations, public relations, and economic project development.  
   
 Ms. Hundley's prior experience and education include:

- Chief Operations Officer and Vice President of Public Affairs for Economic Alliance Houston Port Region
- Director of Community Relations for the Port of Houston Authority
- Regional Manager of Government and Community Relations for Air Products and Chemicals
- Legislative Aide for TX Representative Joe Pickett (D-El Paso)
- Associate District Attorney in El Paso, TX
- National Women’s Council Top 25 Business Women in Houston – 2014
- J.D., University of Texas Law School
- Masters in Strategic Public Relations, The George Washington University, Washington D.C.

For contact and additional information, please visit the AL Law Group's website at [www.allawgp.com](http://www.allawgp.com).
