#### [U.S. and Foreign Pollution](/images/sip/u-s-and-foreign-pollution1.png)A question I would like to ask Gina McCarthy:

> ## **Why does the world get to pollute under the Clean Air Act . . . and then we can pollute to the extent there is room left over?**
>
> ###### (*See generally NAAQS/SIP process at* 42 U.S.C. § 7407(a) *seq*.)

Seems like a crazy way to design a system.  You would think we would have a system that put the U.S. at the front of the line rather than the back.

The world is changing.  We must change with it.  Time to transform the SIP process.  We can make it happen.
