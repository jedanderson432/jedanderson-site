# Venue note—*The Latency Bet*

Prepared 2026-08-03. Supersedes the note written for the previous draft.

**What changed.** The earlier piece carried six theses at ~5,000 words and was engineered so that a figure would spread. This one carries one thesis at ~4,050 words and is engineered so that an argument will. That makes it a better fit for fewer venues and a much better fit for those.

**Length:** ~4,050 words. First draft was 3,479; three hostile reviews forced roughly 1,300 words of substantive correction (the anti-circumvention doctrine, the third term in the exploitability product, the Part 75 non-diffusion answer, the endpoint-scope limit, the independence caveat, the uncosted instrument). Those additions are the argument, not decoration, and cutting them reintroduces errors. Any venue under 3,500 words requires losing a limb rather than trimming.

## The fallback, which is the plan

1. **Canonical publication on jedanderson.org** at `/essays/the-latency-bet`, companion at `/essays/environmental-safety-mode`. Permanent URL, CC-BY-4.0, open fact ledger.
2. **arXiv cs.CY** for the companion. It has the references, the formal treatment and the specification.
3. **The travelling artifact is now the argument, not the figure.** The previous draft was built so that a chart would spread. This one is built so that one sentence does: *environmental law never regulated behaviour, it regulated behaviour at human search speed.* Figure 1 (the proxy stack and the collapsing search cost) supports it and is screenshot-able, but it is no longer carrying the distribution on its own.
4. **`analysis/ESM-spec.md` propagates separately**, forkable by someone who reads neither essay.

**What success means.** One agency adds an attribution field to a deviation report. One lab adds an environmental environment to a reward-hacking benchmark. One permit writer publishes a machine-readable condition. Each is named in the piece, each is cheap, and each is worth more than a masthead.

## What would raise every number here

- **Find the trend.** The strongest objection in all three reviews is that the essay predicts an observable shift in exploitation behaviour and shows none. A quantitative pass over NSR applicability determinations, netting filings, or synthetic-minor structures over twenty-five years would either supply the missing evidence or kill the thesis. Either outcome is publishable, and the second is more valuable.
- **Price the instrument.** The uncosted-instrument objection is conceded in the text and unanswered. A worked cost estimate for continuous physical grading of one medium at one facility class would convert the proposal from an argument into an engineering artifact.
- **Publish the false-trip study.** Still the specification's largest gap.
