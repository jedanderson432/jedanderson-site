# superseded_by proposal (2026-10-26)

Status: **proposal only. Nothing below has been applied.** No essay has `superseded_by` set. Approve groups, edit them, or strike them, and the approved rows become one frontmatter line each.

What setting `superseded_by: {slug}` does: the piece shows a banner ("A newer, clearer version of this argument: {title}") and drops out of the llms.txt Canonical essays list and the homepage Start here and Foundational sections. Its URL, `.md` endpoint, PDF and DOI stay exactly as they are.

Scope: all 110 published essays, grouped by the core argument each one makes. Each group names one proposed canonical piece. Under **Candidates** are the pieces proposed to point at it. Under **Keep** are pieces in the group that do something the canonical piece doesn't (a different form, audience or sub-claim) and so shouldn't carry the banner.

Legend: ★ = already tagged `canonical`. DOI = has a minted DOI (the banner doesn't touch it). ⚠ = contains a stale figure: "compound in information's favor", a 10²⁰ leverage figure, or Λ = Mc² (see the scan at the end).
