# Tag Vocabulary

Subjects are handled via tags, not via subcategories. The same essay may carry 3-7 tags spanning subject, thinker, form, and provenance.

## Subject tags (what is it about)
- enviroai — ESI work, environmental superintelligence
- physics — first-principles physics writing
- information-theory — Landauer, Shannon, computation-as-physics
- thermodynamics — entropy, negentropy, Second Law
- faith — spirituality, meaning, theological integration
- legal-reform — Clean Air Act and regulatory work
- cosmic-ledger — the planetary-threat framing
- causal-sovereignty — Bits Protect Its as a thesis
- holography — holographic principle, AdS/CFT, boundary information

## Thinker tags (engages a specific argument)
- deutsch, wheeler, yudkowsky, whitehead, landauer, bekenstein

## Form tags (what kind of essay within essays)
- cornerstone — the very top tier of anchor pieces, surfaced first on the homepage as "Start here". Use sparingly (3–4 pieces total). A piece tagged `cornerstone` does not need to also be tagged `foundational`; for homepage display cornerstones are excluded from the Foundational treatises section to avoid duplication.
- foundational — anchor pieces; surfaced on the homepage's Foundational treatises section. Pieces also tagged `cornerstone` are excluded from this section (they appear only in "Start here").
- treatise — long-form first-principles synthesis
- paper — formally academic register (the rare case where a piece has abstract + references + co-authors)
- speech — prepared-remarks-as-essay
- letter — addressed-piece-as-essay
- linkedin-original — provenance for posts that originated as LinkedIn content
- email-original — provenance for posts that originated as Constant Contact emails
- visual — pieces where the image is the primary payload

## Conventions
- Aim for 3-7 tags per piece
- Reuse existing tags before inventing new ones
- The `foundational` tag is reserved for genuine anchors of the corpus
